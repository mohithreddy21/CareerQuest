search organized.

# Discover Jobs

## Page headline

**Find jobs worth pursuing.**

## Supporting copy

Search supported sources or paste any public job URL. Every job is
converted into the same structured format so you can compare
opportunities consistently.

## URL import

Label: **Add a job by URL**

Placeholder: **Paste a public job posting URL**

Button: **Import Job**

Processing states: - Fetching job posting... - Extracting job
details... - Normalizing requirements... - Analyzing candidate fit...

Success: **Job imported successfully.**

Failure: **We couldn't extract a job posting from this URL. Try another
public job URL or review the job manually.**

# General UX Copy Principles

Use direct, calm language.

Prefer: - "Review Match" - "Tailor Resume" - "Approve Changes" - "Open
Original Job" - "Mark Interested"

Avoid: - "Let AI do everything!" - "Apply to 1,000 jobs instantly!" -
"Guaranteed better results!" - "Magic AI career boost!"

The product should communicate assistance, not certainty.
